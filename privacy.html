Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Version 1

In short: this site has no server, no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics. It reads what you are playing from Spotify and shows it. Your settings stay in your browser. To show lyrics, song details are sent to the lyrics services listed below. Your own photos and videos never leave your device.

Who runs this

Now Playing is a small personal project, not a company. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Spotify. Contact: the project's GitHub issues page.

What the site reads from your Spotify account

After you log in with Spotify, the page asks Spotify for: your display name and profile picture; what is playing (title, artists, album, cover art, length, position); the next track in your queue; whether the current song is in your Liked Songs; and the playback state. When you press a control it sends the matching command (play, pause, skip, seek). When you tap the heart it adds or removes the song from your Liked Songs. The Liked Songs permission is requested only the first time you use the heart.

What is stored on your device

Everything below stays in your own browser. We cannot see it.

  • Spotify login token (a "refresh token") so you stay logged in, plus short-lived values used during login.
  • Your settings: layout, background style, lyrics on/off, translation, motion and contrast options, city day/night choice, your agreement to these documents, and whether you turned on the backup lyrics source.
  • Cached translations of recently viewed lyrics (about the last 40 songs).
  • Your own backgrounds (photos and videos you add) in your browser's IndexedDB storage. They are never uploaded. Photos are resized and their hidden location and camera data is removed; videos are kept as they are.

The site sets no cookies and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Deleting your data

  • Log out (Settings) removes the login token and every setting, cache and agreement this site saved in your browser.
  • Remove all my backgrounds (Settings, under Yours) deletes your photos and videos from the browser.
  • Logging out does not remove this app's permission at Spotify. To do that, remove "Now Playing" at spotify.com/account/apps.
  • You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time.

Children

This site is not directed at children. You must meet Spotify's minimum age to use a Spotify account. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the version number above will change and you will be asked to agree again.

Questions or requests

Open an issue on the project's GitHub issues page. Because nothing is stored on a server, most requests can be handled by logging out and clearing the data as described above.
