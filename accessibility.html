Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The goal is for Now Playing to be usable by as many people as possible, and to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It has not yet been fully audited, so please read the known issues below.

What is built in

  • Keyboard: every control works from the keyboard. Space plays or pauses, the left and right arrows seek 5 seconds, N and P go to the next or previous track, L likes the song, S turns shuffle on or off, R cycles repeat, M mutes, the up and down arrows change the volume, and Escape closes Settings or a menu. The progress bar is a keyboard slider (arrows, Page Up/Down, Home). On the lyrics, the up and down arrows jump to the previous or next line.
  • Screen readers: the progress bar reports its value, switches and buttons have names and states, new songs and messages are announced politely, and there is a "skip to now playing" link and a main landmark.
  • Visible focus: a high-contrast focus ring shows on every control, over any background.
  • Reduce motion: in Settings (and automatically if your device asks for reduced motion), backgrounds stop moving, video backgrounds pause, and lyrics scroll without easing.
  • High contrast: in Settings, a darker backdrop, solid panels and brighter text.
  • Readable text on changing colors: the backdrop is darkened automatically when an album cover is light, and you can dim your own photo or video background.
  • Zoom: zooming the page and larger touch targets are supported; nothing blocks zoom.

Known limitations

  • Text sizes are set in fixed units, so the site does not follow a browser's "larger text" setting. Zooming the page works.
  • Lyrics are not tagged with their language, so a screen reader may read songs in other languages with the wrong accent.
  • Colors come from album covers, so contrast cannot be guaranteed for every cover; use High contrast or the Dim slider if text is hard to read.
  • Automatic checks and keyboard testing have been done. It has not yet been tested with a range of screen readers on real devices or reviewed by an accessibility specialist.
  • Playback controls depend on Spotify Premium, which is outside our control.

Tell us what is not working

If you hit a barrier, please open an issue on the project's GitHub issues page and describe the device, browser and any assistive technology you use. Accessibility problems are treated as bugs.
